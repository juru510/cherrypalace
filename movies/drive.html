Drive (2011)

"I drive."

My Take: Drive like i'm Ryan Gosling. The og "sigma male" movie. Not to be that guy but I watched this movie before tiktok blew it up. I remember seeing a meme reposted from 4chan of some guy basing his entire personality on this movie and his GF found out. I decided to check it out and I thought yeah this is pretty good. I love the neon aesthetics and beautiful soundtrack. One of my first Ryan Gosling movies.
