Death Note (2017)

"Its Deathnoting time!"

My Take:I think I was 13 when I saw this movie, it was around new years and I saw it on netlfix. The trailer looked cool and I watched it and had no issue with it. Turns out it was about an anime and that the movie was actually bad? Idk I really liked it at that age and after a couple rewatches I really fell in love with the cinematography but could see how dumb the movie is. I really liked the premise so I watched the anime and it was then where I was like ahhh damm this movie is ass ngl. Nevertheless I loved watching it, the lighting, music and cinematography is really good which makes up for the terrible story. This was also made me a margaret qualley enthusiast. She was just so seductive and evil it did something weird to my adolescent mind.

Director: Adam Wingard

Director of Photography: David Tattersall

Production Design: Tom Hammock

Costume Design: Emma Potter
