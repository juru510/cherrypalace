Heat (1995)

"Don't let yourself get attached to anything you are not willing to walk out on in 30 seconds flat if you feel the heat around the corner."

My Take: I watched this movie when I was a junior in high school This was the first time I'd seen Al Pacino in anything other than Scarface, and I was like "damn, he's pretty good." I really appreciated the pacing and character development throughout. The cinematography was also great, along with the sound design. I had just gotten AirPods Pro too, and I was like "shit, this movie sounds amazing." I haven't rewatched it since then, but I remember enjoying it. I do want to rewatch it again soon.

Director: Michael Mann

Director of Photography: Dante Spinotti

Production Design: Neil Spisak

Costume Design: Deborah Lynn Scott

Year: 1995
